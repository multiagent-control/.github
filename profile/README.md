# PRIORA

Research-stage work on constraint-aware decision methods for multi-actuator systems.

PRIORA focuses on a difficult technical problem: what a system should do when physical limits, actuator degradation, saturation, or competing objectives make full command satisfaction impossible.

The project investigates decision-making under constraint conflict in systems with multiple effectors.

## Research Direction

The work explores:

- multi-actuator systems,
- constraint-aware decision logic,
- degraded-operation scenarios,
- priority-aware behavior under physical limits,
- simulation-based experimentation,
- reproducible technical evidence workflows.

The central question is how a technical system can recognize constraint conflict, preserve the most important behavior where possible, and expose the limits of what the available actuators can physically deliver.

Public information is intentionally limited while the technical architecture, evidence package, and intellectual-property strategy are under review.
